Furthermore, Feature #7 (Restricted Changes) ensures phone number modifications are restricted and cannot be altered without written administrative approval.
As detailed in Feature #10 (Campus Geofencing), our system enforces strict physical and operational segregation between Boys' and Girls' hostels, junior wings, and senior dormitories. Cards registered to the Boys' Hostel terminal group will be automatically rejected if inserted into a Girls' Hostel unit, and vice versa. This eliminates unauthorized card sharing and ensures compliance with campus boundary protocols.
